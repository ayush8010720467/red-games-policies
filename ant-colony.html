Ant Colony — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who we are

Ant Colony is published by Red Bricks. Contact: redfortbricks@gmail.com.

What the game stores

The game needs an internet connection to play and has no accounts. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters and settings) is stored only on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Google may back up this progress file to your Google account so it can be restored on a new device; we never receive it.

Analytics and app settings

The game uses Google Firebase. Firebase Analytics collects app usage and performance information (such as screens and levels played, session length, device model, operating system, approximate country and an app-instance identifier) so we can understand how the game is used and improve it. Firebase Remote Config uses a Firebase installation identifier to deliver game settings, such as whether ads are shown. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and diagnostic information (such as the crash stack trace, device model, operating system version and app version) so we can find and fix problems. This data is processed by Google; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction and diagnostic information. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in certain US states), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your device. Backups held by Google follow your Google account settings.

Changes

We will update this page if the game's data practices change.
