Quietude — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

Quietude (“Quietude: Minimal Launcher”) is published by Red Bricks. Contact: redfortbricks@gmail.com.

The short version

Quietude has no ads and no accounts. The apps you use, how long you use them, and your notifications stay on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports help us improve the app, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage data.

What stays on your phone

Quietude is a home screen (launcher). To work, it uses the following on your device only:

  • Your list of installed apps, to show them as a text list, search them, and remember your favourites, folders, renamed and hidden apps.
  • Usage access (optional): which app is open and how long you spent in apps, so the time reminder and app blocking work and so the block screen can show your screen time for the last 7 days.
  • Display over other apps (optional): to show the “how long?” question, time-up reminders and the blocked-app screen on top of the app you opened.
  • Notification access (optional): when you switch the notification filter on, notifications from apps you have not allowed are removed from the shade and kept in Quietude's inbox (title, text, time and app). Up to 300 are stored, and you can clear them at any time.
  • Accessibility service, “precision mode” (optional, off unless you switch it on): it is told only which app has just come to the front, so reminders and blocks start instantly. It cannot read or see what is on your screen, it does not record anything, and what it learns is never sent anywhere.
  • Colour correction (optional, “black and white”): with a one-time permission you grant from a computer, Quietude switches the screen to grayscale while chosen apps are open.
  • Your settings, including the date you first opened Quietude (for the 7-day Pro preview).

If Android backup is on, Google may back up Quietude's settings to your Google account so they can be restored on a new phone; we never receive them.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

Quietude uses Google Firebase. Firebase Analytics records how Quietude itself is used: which of its screens and introduction steps you see, which of its features and settings you use, and whether you have granted its permissions, along with device model, Android version, approximate country and an app-instance identifier. It never records which apps you use, app names, notification contents or anything you type. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and diagnostic information (such as the stack trace, device model, Android version and app version) so we can fix problems. This data is processed by Google; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. We do not use it to identify you. You can switch both off in Settings → Share anonymous usage data.

Messages from us

We may occasionally send a notification through Firebase Cloud Messaging, for example to tell you an update fixes a problem. To deliver these, Firebase uses an installation identifier and the topics the app subscribes to (all users, and your app version). You can turn these notifications off in Android's settings for Quietude.

Purchases

Quietude Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Pro, and remembers the answer on your phone. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time in Google Play.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. The only third party involved is Google, as described above.

Children

Quietude is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Everything Quietude stores on your phone is deleted when you uninstall it or clear its storage. To ask about analytics or crash data, email us at the address above.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
